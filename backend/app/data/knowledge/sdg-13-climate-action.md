## What SDG 13 commits to

Sustainable Development Goal 13 calls for urgent action to combat climate change
and its impacts. Its formal targets cover strengthening resilience and adaptive
capacity to climate-related hazards, integrating climate measures into national
policy and planning, improving education and awareness on mitigation and
adaptation, and mobilising finance — with a specific commitment to the needs of
developing countries and small island states.

## Why it is framed as a goal of its own

Climate change is a multiplier. It worsens water scarcity, food insecurity,
health outcomes and poverty, so progress on many other goals depends on whether
warming is limited. Conversely, many climate actions — cleaner energy, better
transit, restored ecosystems — deliver health and economic benefits immediately,
independently of their long-term benefit.

## What organisations and individuals can contribute

- **Mitigation:** reduce emissions from energy use, transport, procurement and
  waste; shift to lower-carbon options where they are available.
- **Adaptation:** identify local climate risks and reduce exposure to them
  before an event occurs.
- **Education:** make climate literacy part of ordinary training, not a special
  event.
- **Disclosure:** measure and publish emissions honestly, including the parts
  that are not improving.
- **Advocacy:** influence the systems — grid, transit, building codes — that set
  the default for everyone.

## What progress means

Progress is not the same as a lower reported number. Genuine progress requires
that reductions are real (not displaced elsewhere), durable, and measured
against a stated baseline. Targets should be transparent about scope and about
what has been excluded.

## A note on equity

Responsibility for emissions and vulnerability to their effects are distributed
unequally. Climate action that ignores this can shift burdens onto those least
able to carry them, which is why the goal explicitly foregrounds resilience and
support for the most exposed communities.
