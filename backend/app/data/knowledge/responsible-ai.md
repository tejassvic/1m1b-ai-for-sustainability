## Transparency

Verdant shows the sources behind every answer. Retrieved documents are cited
with their publisher so a reader can check them, and the language model in use
is named in the response metadata. When an answer is produced without a language
model — for example when no model credentials are configured — the response says
so rather than disguising a template as a generated answer.

## Privacy

The assistant does not require an account and does not store conversations on the
server. Chat history lives in the browser for the duration of the visit and is
discarded when the page is closed. Location coordinates are accepted only as
optional context for locally relevant guidance; they are not logged, not
persisted and not used to identify an individual. No personally identifiable
information is required to use any feature.

## Grounded AI

Responses about sustainability are built from a curated knowledge base. The
language model is instructed to use the supplied passages, to avoid inventing
figures or citations, and to indicate when the retrieved material does not
answer the question. It is explicitly forbidden from producing the numeric
results of the Impact Analyzer — those come from deterministic calculation.

## Human agency

Verdant informs decisions; it does not make them. Output is framed as guidance
and clearly distinguished from official or regulatory advice. Every
recommendation is a suggestion with a stated rationale, so a person can evaluate
it rather than merely comply.

## Honest uncertainty

Estimates are labelled as estimates. Ranges are not collapsed into false
precision, the categories excluded from a calculation are named, and the absence
of data is never presented as evidence of low impact. Where local conditions
could change the answer substantially, the response says so.

## What Verdant does not do

- It does not issue warnings, alerts or predictions about specific events.
- It does not present model output as a measurement or a guarantee.
- It does not store personal data or build behavioural profiles.
- It does not claim environmental outcomes it cannot evidence.
